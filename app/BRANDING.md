# PHANTOM Branding

## Project name

The official name of the project is **PHANTOM**.

## "Bláthy On Top"

The smaller line displayed under the name in the terminal reads:

> Bláthy On Top

This is a **personal reference** by the original author to their school, the
**Bláthy Ottó Titusz Informatikai Technikum**.

- It is **not** the official slogan of PHANTOM.
- It does **not** mean that the school develops, endorses, sponsors or supports the project.
- PHANTOM is an **independently developed** project and is **not official school software**.
- The name "Bláthy" appears solely as a personal reference.
